Yes — Spark is free forever, with monthly credits across our core writing and image tools. No credit card required to start.

Verify a valid school email address in your account settings to get 30% off the Creator or Pro plan, for as long as you're enrolled or teaching.

The Team plan is built for that — it includes shared workspaces, per-seat billing, and admin controls to manage everyone in one place.
